HOW TO PLAY

  • Touch / mouse: press and drag to create one neon ribbon. Release to let it fade.
  • Colored comets score only in the matching moon well at the bottom.
  • Wrong wells, missed comets, and eclipse stones damage integrity.
  • Chain correct catches for streak sparks and bonus points.

DESIGN NOTES

The game turns simple mobile drawing into a precision-speed tradeoff: broad swipes are safe but wasteful, short angled ribbons are risky but satisfying. Visual feedback favors readable color lanes, impact blooms, and a breathing neon garden mood.
