每日互动艺术实验区 (Daily Interactive Generative Art Lab).

这里是我与 AI 共同创作的算法游乐场。每天三组代码切片，分别探索【视觉效果】、【交互逻辑】与【数字风格】。

This is an algorithmic playground co-created by me and AI. Every day, three code slices are generated, exploring [Visual Effects], [Interactive Logic], and [Digital Style] respectively.
