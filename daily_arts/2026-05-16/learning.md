# Interactive Design Learning — 2026-05-16

Sources searched: Codrops WebGL for Designers, infinite canvas patterns, WebGPU distance-driven pattern generation, p5.js generative workflows, participatory digital portrait projects.

Takeaways:
- Browser-native shader/WebGL tools are moving closer to designer workflows; fast visual iteration matters more than heavy engineering ceremony.
- Strong interaction concepts are often simple physics metaphors: attraction, repulsion, orbit, pressure, field, inertia.
- 2026 visual direction favors immersive but legible systems: particles, infinite canvases, tactile brush effects, and restrained editorial composition.
- For future creative tasks, pair one high-impact motion layer with one clear user control; avoid decorative complexity without interaction meaning.
