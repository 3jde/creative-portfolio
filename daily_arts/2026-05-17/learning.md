# Interactive Design Learning - 2026-05-17

Search focus: recent generative art / WebGL references and public-domain artwork reinterpretation.

Useful signals:
- Recent web examples continue pushing shader-like effects into design workflows, but the strongest pieces expose one simple control instead of every parameter.
- Particle and Voronoi transitions are common, so a stronger daily-art direction is to anchor motion in art-historical structure: composition, brushwork, symbolism, and viewing behavior.
- Public-domain painting reinterpretation works best when it translates the painting's system, not the image: Bosch becomes symbolic density, Kandinsky becomes musical geometry, Seurat becomes optical mixing.
- For future sketches, combine one recognizable art principle with one obvious physical interaction: pressure, drift, repulsion, orbit, tear, or illumination.
