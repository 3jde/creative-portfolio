# Interactive Design Learning - 2026-05-21

Search focus: current web interaction signals that can improve daily generative art.

Useful signals:
- Recent design trend writing continues to emphasize kinetic typography, CSS/texture depth, and interaction that carries the main narrative instead of decorating it.
- WebGL inspiration roundups are useful, but the stronger lesson for this series is restraint: make motion legible through one clear metaphor before adding visual density.
- For painting reinterpretation, translating the work's hidden mechanics remains stronger than surface mimicry: Rembrandt becomes directed light, Vermeer becomes gaze and pearl reflection, Hiroshige becomes weather pressure.
- Future sketches should keep using familiar input metaphors on mobile: cursor/touch as lamp, lens, brush, wind, rain, or gravity.
