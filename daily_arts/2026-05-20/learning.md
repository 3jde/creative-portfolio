# Interactive Design Learning - 2026-05-20

Search focus: recent interactive/generative art signals plus public-domain painting reinterpretation.

Useful signals:
- Recent examples keep pairing generative motion with familiar input metaphors: cursor as brush, pressure as field, drag as force.
- Stronger results come from translating a painting's operating system rather than copying its surface image: Turner becomes velocity, Botticelli becomes wind, Monet becomes optical mixing.
- For future creative tasks, avoid generic particle fields by making the interaction legible as part of the artwork's subject: storm pressure, shell breath, water reflection.
- Current visual trends still favor immersive WebGL and AI-assisted experimentation, but the daily-art series should keep controls simple and compositional intent clear.
