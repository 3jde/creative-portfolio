# Interactive Design Learning - 2026-06-05

Primary MANA references:
- https://m.manamana.net/ActivityDetail/806 - *HiTree: Our Forest*, a compound interactive installation.
- https://m.manamana.net/activityDetail/1051 - digital preservation and interactive display in museums.
- https://m.manamana.net/ActivityDetail/727 - Web3D/XR immersive interaction design.

Key learning:
- Treat participation as authorship. A visitor-drawn route should remain visible and govern later motion, rather than trigger a disposable effect.
- Build immersion through a coherent spatial rule, not visual density alone. Piranesi's contradiction is stronger when every stair belongs to one rotating world.
- Keep one gesture legible on mobile. Draw, tilt, and open are direct verbs that connect input to concept.
- Preserve quiet intervals. The Blake study uses sparse arcs and slow pulses so attention can settle between events.

Application: future pieces should retain user traces longer, use collective behavior after input, and reserve complex rendering for concepts that genuinely depend on depth.
