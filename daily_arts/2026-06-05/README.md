# Daily Interactive Art - 2026-06-05

Source references:
- Giovanni Battista Piranesi, *Carceri d'invenzione* - transformed into a rotating WebGL architecture of contradictory stairs, wells, and drifting sparks.
- Pieter Bruegel the Elder, *Hunters in the Snow* - transformed into a winter field where a drawn route becomes the skaters' collective choreography.
- William Blake, *The Ancient of Days* - transformed into a restrained celestial measuring instrument of compass legs, arcs, and star dust.

Sketches:
- `visual.html`: move to tilt the impossible prison; press to accelerate the falling light.
- `interactive.html`: drag across the ice to draw a route; double tap/click to clear it.
- `artistic.html`: move horizontally to open the compass; tap to reseed the cosmos.

Phone viewing:
1. Run `python3 -m http.server 8080` from the workspace.
2. Open `http://<computer-lan-ip>:8080/daily_art/2026-06-05/` on the phone.

Anti-repetition: no particle field as primary structure, no grid, no orbit ecology, no pressure waves, no draggable object assembly, and no atmospheric landscape. The works use architectural contradiction, gesture-authored choreography, and geometric contemplation respectively.
