# Interactive Design Learning — 2026-05-08

Key takeaways from current searches:
- 2026 interface direction emphasizes generative UI, AI personalization, spatial/3D layers, micro-interactions, and multimodal/touch-first control.
- Good interactive art should expose one clear gesture immediately, then reward deeper play with emergent complexity.
- For mobile-first creative sketches: large touch targets, low text load, strong contrast, and graceful performance matter more than maximal detail.
- Applied today: visual piece uses spatial depth; interaction piece centers touch gravity; artistic piece favors restrained composition and instant reseeding.
