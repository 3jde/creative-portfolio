# Interactive Design Learning - 2026-05-22

Search focus: public-domain painting sources and current generative-art framing.

Useful signals:
- Public-domain source roundups remain valuable because they encourage direct study of composition systems rather than generic mood-board borrowing.
- The best translation move is still structural: Las Meninas becomes perspective and mirror authority, The Scream becomes pressure propagation, The Kiss becomes ornament growth.
- For future daily sketches, keep pairing a famous artwork with a single legible input metaphor. Users understand touch as lamp, pressure, brush, gravity, growth, or lens without instruction-heavy UI.
- Avoid treating WebGL density as the default answer. A flat 2D system can be stronger when the artwork is about pattern, rhythm, or surface.
