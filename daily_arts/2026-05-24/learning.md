# Interactive Design Learning - 2026-05-24

Search focus: MANA / Manamana new-media examples, TouchDesigner learning signals, and museum-grade interaction patterns.

Useful signals:
- MANA examples emphasize art/design/technology as one production system, especially interaction projection, light effects, installation art, and immersive public experiences.
- The TouchDesigner forum and generative-poster learning examples point toward parameterized systems: expose a few meaningful controls, then let visual complexity emerge from motion, repetition, and feedback.
- For painting reinterpretation, choose a clear structural metaphor before adding motion. Uccello becomes perspective vectors, Arcimboldo becomes movable component identity, and Whistler becomes tonal atmosphere.
- Mobile-first interaction works best when one gesture owns the sketch: orbit/drag for spatial tension, drag/release for physical assembly, pointer drift for ambient works.
