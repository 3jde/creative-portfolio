Solar Reliquary

Three p5.js transformations of public-domain paintings: Turner as a WebGL light engine, Delacroix as an input-driven crowd physics field, and Malevich as a quiet generative timing system.

Visual ImpactTurner: storm light, chromatic vortices, pointer-bent camera depth. Novel InteractionDelacroix: press keys and drag to rally, scatter, and rebuild a symbolic crowd. Artistic QualityMalevich: spare geometry, temporal drift, restraint, and calibrated imbalance.
